Why we started GB$

We needed a fair way to make family work visible.

GoodbodyBucks did not start as a startup idea. It started as a family system: one board, nine kids, clear rules, and real accountability. The board made invisible work visible. It showed whose turn it was, what contribution was expected, and what privileges had to be earned.

VisibilityEveryone can see the rules.
RotationWork gets shared fairly.
ClarityNo guessing who owes what.

GB$ continues that legacy for modern families, adding kid wallets, screen-time choices, learning rewards, and parent controls without losing the original lesson: contribution comes before consumption.

How it works today

💰 Kids earn GB$ for chores, learning & outdoor work
🍕 Spend on food, screen time & real privileges
⏱️ Screen time tracked by a live countdown timer
⚠️ Consequences for poor choices — money or time
📚 Summer Academy built in
🔒 Parents control every rule
